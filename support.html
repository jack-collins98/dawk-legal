Common Questions

How do I delete my account?
Open the app, go to Profile → Account Settings → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and data.

I found a bug. What should I include when reporting it?
Please let us know what device and iOS version you're using, and the steps to reproduce the issue. Screenshots help a lot!

How do I report inappropriate content or a user?
Use the report option available on posts and comments within the app, or email us directly with details.
